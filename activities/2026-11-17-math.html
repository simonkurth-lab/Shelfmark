1. Base and exponent

The big number 10 is the base.
The small raised number 3 is the exponent.
Write the base 3 times and multiply.

2. Expand a power

103 = 10 × 10 × 10
10 × 10 = 100
100 × 10 = 1,000
So 103 = 1,000

5. Careful! 103 is NOT 10 × 3.
10 × 3 = 30 (add 10 three times). 103 = 10 × 10 × 10 = 1,000 (multiply three 10s).
