Daily Math: Multiplying by Powers of 10

Last time you learned 103 = 1,000. Today we multiply other numbers by powers of 10. Same pattern: the exponent tells you how many zeros to add! 💪

The pattern:

Write the number, then add as many zeros as the exponent.
Example: 35 × 103 = 35 then 3 zeros = 35,000.
Careful: if the number already ends in zero, keep it. 40 × 102 = 40 then 2 zeros = 4,000.
