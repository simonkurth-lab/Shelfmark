Place Value Rescue: Fix-It Practice

Mistakes help us learn! Go one part at a time, read the examples, and use the hints. 💪

📌 Part A: Digit, place, or value?

Read the question carefully. These are three different questions:

  • What digit? Answer with just the digit. Example: 2
  • What place? Answer with a place name. Example: hundredths
  • What value? Answer with a number. Example: 0.02 (never a word like "tens")

Digit: 3 · Place: hundredths · Value: 3 × 0.01 = 0.03

Place: tens · Value: 8 × 10 = 80

📌 Part B: Expanded form with decimals

Each digit becomes its value. Line up each part with its place.

3.257 = 3 + 0.2 + 0.05 + 0.007 (ones + tenths + hundredths + thousandths)

Example: 5.384 = 5 + 0.3 + 0.08 + 0.004

Which digit is missing? It is the 5, in the hundredths place.

📌 Part C: Finding 1/10 of a number

1/10 of a number means divide by 10. Every digit moves one place to the right, so the answer gets smaller.

Check yourself: Is your answer smaller than the number you started with? If it got bigger, you multiplied by mistake.

Example: 1/10 of 300 → 300 ÷ 10 = 30 (30 is smaller than 300 ✓)

💡 Need a hint?

Divide by 10. Every digit moves one place to the right.

📌 Part D: Rounding

  1. Underline the digit in the rounding place.
  2. Look next door: the digit just to its right.
  3. 5 or more: the underlined digit goes up 1. 4 or less: it stays the same.
  4. Whole numbers: digits to the right become 0. Decimals: digits to the right drop off.

3.847 to the nearest tenth: 3.847 → next door is 4 → stay → 3.8 (not 3.800 or 3.850)
