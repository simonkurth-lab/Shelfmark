📌 Part A: Finding 1/10 of a number

1/10 of a number means divide by 10. Every digit moves one place to the right, so the answer gets smaller.

Check yourself: Is your answer smaller than the number you started with?

Examples: 1/10 of 300 = 30 · 1/10 of 2,000 = 200 · 1/10 of 50 = 5
