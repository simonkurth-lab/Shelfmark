Daily Math: Decimals on a Number Line

Fourteen questions. Number lines are just rulers for decimals. Count the jumps carefully! 💪

📌 Part A: Counting in tenths

Between two whole numbers there are 10 tenths. Counting in tenths from 2.1 goes: 2.1, 2.2, 2.3 … 2.8, 2.9, and then 3.0.

Khalid started a number line at 2.1 and counted in tenths. He labeled it:

2.1 · 2.2 · 2.3 · 2.4 · 2.5 · 2.6 · 2.7 · 2.8 · 2.9 · 2.10 · 2.11

💡 Need a hint?

Count in tenths: 2.8, 2.9, then you reach the next whole number.

💡 Need a hint?

It is one more tenth after the whole number.

Sophie says: "If I start counting at 3 and count along in jumps of 3 tenths, I will say the number 4.1."
