📖 Worked example

What is the value of the 5 in 2.856?

  1. Find the 5. It is the second digit after the decimal point.
  2. Check the chart: the second place after the point is hundredths.
  3. 5 hundredths = 0.05. That's the answer!

🔤 Words to know

Digit
One of the symbols 0–9 that we use to write numbers.
Place value
What a digit is worth because of where it sits in a number.
Expanded form
A number written as the sum of what each digit is worth. 352 = 300 + 50 + 2.
Decimal
A number with a decimal point that shows parts of a whole, like 3.5.
Tenths
1st place after the point. 10 tenths make 1 whole.
Hundredths
2nd place after the point. 100 hundredths make 1 whole.
Thousandths
3rd place after the point. 1,000 thousandths make 1 whole.
