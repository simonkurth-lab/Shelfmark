Daily Math: Powers of 10 and Exponents

Ten questions. Powers of 10 have a cool pattern: just count the zeros! 💪

📌 Part A: Meet the exponent

An exponent is a small raised number. It tells you how many times to write the base (the big number) in a multiplication.

10³ = 10 × 10 × 10 = 1,000

Here the base is 10 and the exponent is 3, so we multiply three 10s.

The pattern:

10¹ = 10 (one zero)
10² = 100 (two zeros)
10³ = 1,000 (three zeros)
10⁴ = 10,000 (four zeros)

The exponent matches the number of zeros!
