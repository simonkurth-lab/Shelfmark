Have you ever had to be brave when you were scared? In Finding Nemo, a nervous clownfish named Marlin swims across the ocean to rescue his son, Nemo, after a diver takes him. Along the way, Marlin faces many challenges, but he overcomes them with courage, trust, and friendship.

The first challenge Marlin faces is his own fear. Ever since a barracuda attacked his family, Marlin has been afraid of everything in the ocean. He doesn’t even want Nemo to go to school. When Nemo is taken, Marlin has to leave the safety of the reef and swim into the open sea. He overcomes his fear by thinking about Nemo and pushing forward, even when he meets sharks.

Another challenge Marlin faces is learning to trust others. Marlin wants to control everything himself. Inside the whale, Dory tells him to let go, and he is terrified. When he finally trusts her, the whale shoots them out of its blowhole and close to Sydney, where Nemo is. Marlin learns that he can’t do everything alone.

The biggest challenge Marlin faces is letting Nemo be brave on his own. At the end, Dory gets caught in a fishing net, and Nemo wants to swim in to help. Marlin is scared he will lose Nemo again. Instead of saying no, he tells Nemo, “I know you can do this.” Nemo saves Dory and the other fish.

In Finding Nemo, Marlin faces his fears, learns to trust, and lets his son grow up. These challenges change him from a worried dad into a brave one. The movie shows that even when life is scary, we can overcome challenges with courage and help from friends.
