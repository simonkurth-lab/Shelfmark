📖 What happens in this chapter

Esperanza prays at the shrine, and Isabel prays to be named Queen of the May. Isabel has straight A's, but Josefina says the school always picks a blonde, blue-eyed queen.

Miguel loses his job as a mechanic. Men from Oklahoma offered to work for half the wages, so the railroad hired them. Esperanza gets angry and argues with Miguel. She says cruel things, and the next morning he is gone.

Isabel is not chosen as Queen. Esperanza gives her the porcelain doll from her valise. Mama comes home from the hospital and says Esperanza is mature. Then Esperanza finds her money orders missing.

🔤 Words to know

shrine
A special place where people pray.
prejudice
Judging people in a bad way because of who they are, not what they do.
unfair
Not fair. Someone is treated worse for no good reason.
wages
The money a worker is paid for a job.
strike
When workers stop working to ask for better pay or better treatment.
valise
A small suitcase.
mature
Acting grown-up and thinking carefully.
money order
A paper that works like a check. You can send money with it.
