Decimals: Compare, Order & Round

Take it one question at a time. Use the hints whenever you need them. 💪

📖 Worked example

Which is greater: 0.6 or 0.58?

  1. Line up the decimal points: 0.60 and 0.58.
  2. Compare the tenths first: 6 tenths is more than 5 tenths.
  3. So 0.6 is greater. Adding a zero doesn't change a decimal's value!
