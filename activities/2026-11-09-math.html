Daily Math: 1/10 of a Number, Now with Decimals

Ten questions. Last time 1/10 of a number made it smaller. Today the answer can land between whole numbers! 💪

📌 Part A: 1/10 of a number (whole numbers and decimals)

1/10 of a number still means divide by 10. Every digit moves one place to the right. When a digit moves past the ones place, it lands in the tenths place after the decimal point.

Examples: 1/10 of 80 = 8 · 1/10 of 8 = 0.8 · 1/10 of 0.8 = 0.08

Check yourself: Picture it on a number line. Is your answer closer to 0 than the number you started with?

📌 Part B: Expanded form with decimals

Decimal places have values too: tenths (0.1), hundredths (0.01).

Example: 5.28 = 5 + 0.2 + 0.08

Example: 3.04 = 3 + 0.04 (no tenths, so skip it)
