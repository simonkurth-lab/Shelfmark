Use the number line picture to help you on every question. Take your time! 💪

Reading a number line:

  • Count the equal parts between 0 and 1. That is the bottom number (denominator).
  • Count the marks from 0 to the point. That is the top number (numerator).
  • 10 parts = tenths (0.1 each). 100 parts = hundredths (0.01 each).

Farther right = greater.
