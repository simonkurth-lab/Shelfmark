🔤 Words to know

unfair
Not fair. Someone is treated worse for no good reason.
prejudice
Judging people in a bad way because of who they are, not what they do.
strike
When workers stop working to ask for better pay or better treatment.
raid
A sudden visit by officials to search a place or take people away.
deport
To send a person out of the country.
citizen
A person who legally belongs to a country.
valise
A small suitcase.
